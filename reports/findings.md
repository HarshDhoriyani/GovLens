# Demonstration Findings

> **Synthetic data only.** These descriptive observations apply only to this generated demonstration dataset; they are not evidence about real districts, public agencies, or government performance.

## Observations

1. The dataset contains **6,000** requests; 3,874 completed, 958 pending, 738 in progress, and 430 rejected.
2. Completion rate is **69.6%** (completed divided by completed + pending + in-progress; rejected cases are excluded).
3. **Aster** has the most requests (1,250); **Aster** has the lowest completion rate (60.7%) and **Fern** the highest (80.0%).
4. **Water Supply** has the highest volume (1,362); **Roads and Infrastructure** has the largest pending + in-progress backlog (408).
5. In the final two months, volume was higher: 246 in 2025-11 and 264 in 2025-12. Completed requests with valid resolution times averaged 8.9 days (median 7.0 days).

## Follow-up questions, not causal conclusions

- Investigate the synthetic lower-rate and high-backlog segments by request age, staffing, and service complexity before proposing operational changes.
- Check whether category-specific resolution-time differences persist after accounting for case mix and submission month.
- Review long-pending cases and define service-level targets before interpreting backlogs as delays.
- No intervention was tested; the observed patterns are descriptive and do not establish causes.
